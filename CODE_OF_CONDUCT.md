# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want this community to be welcoming, constructive and useful for everyone.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback specific and evidence-based.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.
- Use the correct route for bugs, support and security reports.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Spam, unrelated promotion, or deceptive content.
- Publishing credentials, private data, licence keys, or vulnerability details.
- Attempts to pressure others into sharing private information.

## Moderation
Maintainers may edit, close, lock or remove content that breaches these rules, is off-topic, duplicates an existing report, or creates a safety risk. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to blocking.

To report a conduct concern, use the private support route on [writrex.com](https://writrex.com/).
