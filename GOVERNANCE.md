# Community Governance

Writrex maintainers manage this repository and make final decisions on moderation, roadmap priorities and documentation.

## Triage
New reports may be labelled, asked for clarification, moved to a more suitable category, closed as duplicate, or closed as not planned.

## Product decisions
Feedback is valuable, but a discussion or request does not guarantee implementation. Priority depends on impact, safety, reliability, product direction and available evidence.

## Transparency
We will keep public roadmap notes high-level. Private commercial, customer, security and operational details will not be discussed publicly.
