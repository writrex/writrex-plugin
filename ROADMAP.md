# Writrex Plugin Roadmap

This is a high-level public view of direction, not a promise or delivery date.

## Now
- Supporting the released Writrex Plugin.
- Collecting real-world feedback, bug reports and usability observations.

## Next
- Prioritising the most valuable reliability, guidance and workflow improvements from community feedback.
- Improving public documentation where repeated questions show a gap.

## Exploring
- Quality-of-life improvements suggested by customers.
- Integrations or workflows that clearly benefit the plugin's users.

## Shipped
- Writrex Plugin is released and available to purchase.

## How priorities are decided
We consider user impact, reproducibility, security, product fit and the number of people affected. A request may be closed or moved when it is already covered, not planned, or needs more detail.
